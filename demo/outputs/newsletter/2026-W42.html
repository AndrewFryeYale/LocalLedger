Methodology. Local Ledger ingests public records (SEC filings, building permits, municipal agendas, bank call reports, state production and gaming data), maps them to 237 under-covered US microcaps, and ranks signals by rule-based significance blended with company priority. Analyst agents write each item; a fact-check pass drops any claim not supported verbatim or numerically by its cited document. Each company also has a model built from its filings; "So what for our model" lines show how a signal moves our revenue estimates (ranges, P(up y/y)) and value sensitivity at the current market multiple.
