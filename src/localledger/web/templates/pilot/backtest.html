Method and limits

  • Forecasts use only quarters public before the forecast date (the filing date, or at the latest 95 days after quarter end for values that reached XBRL through a later filing). Curated event overlays are excluded because they were written after the fact.
  • Event returns start from the close before the filing date, so they include the first-day reaction; a reader acting on the flag the next morning would see the remaining move. Microcap prices are noisy and these are medians of small samples; this is not a trading strategy.
  • Prices: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, for internal research only (see LEGAL_REVIEW.md, question 26).
  • Reproduce with ll backfill-agendas then ll backtest; results in out/backtest/.
