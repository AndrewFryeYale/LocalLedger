# PCYO: Pure Cycle. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation. The war-room Thesis agent tests each print against it.

## Thesis
- Sky Ranch (Arapahoe County, CO) is a multi-phase master-planned community. Each phase converts into lot sales to national homebuilders plus water and wastewater tap fees (10-Q: $37.6M of tap fees received through May 31, 2026; Phase 2 additional tap fees estimated at more than $19.0M).
- Lot deliveries are paced annually to builder absorption, which limits inventory carry (Q3 FY26 Ex. 99.1).
- The water segment has a second, non-housing demand source: water sales to oil and gas operators drove the Q3 FY26 water revenue increase.
- A single-family rental portfolio adds recurring revenue (95% of units leased before completion per Q3 FY26 Ex. 99.1).

## Drivers and public data
- Census BPS: Arapahoe County single-family permits (tap timing depends on when homebuilders request building permits).
- Arapahoe County / Sky Ranch plat and permit approvals (agenda watchlist: "Sky Ranch", "Pure Cycle", "Rangeview").
- 8-K Ex. 99.1 and 10-Q: lots delivered, phase completion %, taps sold, acre-feet delivered, oil and gas water sales.

## Kill criteria
- Phase 2D or 2E completion slips by two or more quarters versus the timing stated in the prior release.
- Taps sold in a phase stall (no change over two consecutive quarters) while Arapahoe County single-family permits fall more than 25% year over year.
- Water and wastewater revenue declines year over year for two quarters as oil and gas water demand fades.
- Equity raise or new debt to fund development beyond the existing facilities.

## Open questions
- Outcome of the August 2026 activist proxy contest and any change to capital allocation.
- Builder contracts for Phase 2E lots (not yet signed for all builders per the Q3 FY26 release).
