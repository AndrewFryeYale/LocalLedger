# CPHC: Canterbury Park. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation.

## Thesis
- Minnesota's only live pari-mutuel thoroughbred and quarter horse racetrack, plus "unbanked" card casino, in Shakopee, about 20 miles from downtown Minneapolis (10-K).
- The underappreciated asset is surplus land around the racetrack (Canterbury Commons), developed with City of Shakopee tax increment financing (TIF). Each JV or land sale converts land to cash or recurring income.
- State policy is the swing factor: Minnesota Racing Commission decisions, purse/marketing agreements, and sports-betting legislation.

## Drivers and public data
- City of Shakopee council and EDA agendas (TIF, Canterbury Commons projects, public improvements).
- Minnesota Racing Commission minutes; Minnesota legislative session (sports betting, historical racing).
- 10-Q/8-K: card casino revenue, pari-mutuel revenue, land/JV transactions.

## Kill criteria
- Minnesota legalizes sports betting or expands gaming in a way that excludes the racetrack.
- Card casino revenue declines year over year for three consecutive quarters.
- Canterbury Commons development stalls (no new JV, land sale or TIF action in four quarters).

## Open questions
- Recent Schedule 13D (October 2026) filer intentions.
- Timing of TIF reimbursements and the next development phase.
