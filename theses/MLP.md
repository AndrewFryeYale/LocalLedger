# MLP: Maui Land & Pineapple. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation.

## Thesis
- Large entitled and agricultural land position in West and Upcountry Maui. Value is released through entitlements and land sales (primary and workforce housing, farm lots, resort development) under the 2026 segment structure (Land Development and Sales; Commercial Real Estate Leasing; and others).
- About 247,000 leasable sq ft (Kapalua Resort, Haliimaile Town Center) provides recurring leasing revenue.
- Ditch, reservoir and well systems serving West and Upcountry Maui: water assets and the strategic review of the water business are a potential value-unlock event.

## Drivers and public data
- Maui County Council, Planning Commission and Board of Water Supply agendas (entitlements, Pulelehua, Kapalua; water-use decisions).
- State Commission on Water Resource Management decisions (West Maui surface water).
- Census BPS for Maui County (post-wildfire rebuilding demand).

## Kill criteria
- County or state water decisions materially restrict MLP's water systems or impose uncompensated obligations.
- Land sales fail to materialize for four consecutive quarters while leasing revenue declines.
- Strategic review of water assets ends without a transaction and with added cost.

## Open questions
- Timing and structure of any water-asset transaction; regulatory approvals needed.
