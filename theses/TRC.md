# TRC: Tejon Ranch. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation.

## Thesis
- Value sits in entitled land on about 270,000 acres along I-5 in Kern and LA counties. Monetization comes through the Tejon Ranch Commerce Center (TRCC) industrial/commercial platform, joint-venture land sales, and eventually the residential communities (Mountain Village, Centennial, Grapevine).
- Cost discipline is visible: year-to-date corporate expenses fell to $4.7M from $9.1M (Q2 2026 Ex. 99.1), and recurring segments (multifamily, mineral resources, ranch operations) grew.
- The TRCC industrial JV portfolio of 2.8M sq ft was 100% leased, with Building 1B (about 510,500 sq ft) delivering in early 2027.
- Centennial re-entitlement before LA County is the key optionality event in 2026.

## Drivers and public data
- LA County Regional Planning Commission and Board of Supervisors agendas (Centennial re-entitlement, RPDEIR).
- Kern County Planning Commission and Board agendas (TRCC, Grapevine).
- Census BPS for Kern County; 8-K Ex. 99.1: segment revenues, occupancy at TRCC and Terra Vista, land sales.

## Kill criteria
- Centennial re-entitlement fails at the LA County Board of Supervisors, or new CEQA litigation is filed and an injunction granted.
- Terra Vista leasing falls back below 80%, or TRCC industrial occupancy drops below 95%.
- Corporate expenses re-inflate above the 2025 run-rate excluding one-offs.
- Water-sale or land-sale dependence: two consecutive quarters where net income is positive only because of non-recurring land/water sales.

## Open questions
- Timing of the LA County hearings and the scope of any litigation settlement conditions.
- Next JV land sale at TRCC after Dedeaux.
