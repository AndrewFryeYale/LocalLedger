# AII: American Integrity Insurance. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation.

## Thesis
- Florida-focused personal residential property insurer (Florida was 93.7% of policies in force and 96.5% of in-force premium at Dec 31, 2025; 10-K), and the sixth-largest writer by policies in force per Florida OIR data.
- Florida's 2022–2023 tort and claims reforms improved the market's loss environment. Rate adequacy is set through filings with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation.
- Growth comes partly from Citizens Property Insurance depopulation takeouts.

## Drivers and public data
- Florida OIR rate filings and decisions (SERFF), market-share reports, and Citizens takeout approvals.
- Hurricane season events and reinsurance renewals (June 1).
- 10-Q: gross premiums written, policies in force, loss and expense ratios.

## Kill criteria
- OIR disapproves or materially cuts a requested rate change while loss trends rise.
- A catastrophe event exhausts retention and reinsurance layers, or reinsurance costs rise faster than rate.
- Legislative rollback of the 2022–2023 reforms.

## Open questions
- Concentration in specific Florida counties; reinsurance tower details for 2026–27.
