# SDHC: Smith Douglas Homes. Internal thesis draft

> Internal working draft, seeded from public filings for Andrew's review. It isn't published research and isn't a recommendation.

## Thesis
- Entry-level and empty-nest single-family builder in southeastern and southern metros (Atlanta, Birmingham, Central Georgia, Charlotte, Chattanooga, Dallas-Fort Worth, Greenville, Houston, Huntsville, Nashville, Raleigh, Alabama Gulf Coast).
- A land-light model: finished lots bought via lot-option contracts from third-party developers and land bankers, so returns depend on absorption and option-lot pipeline rather than land appreciation.
- Local permitting and lot approvals in suburban counties drive closings with a lag.

## Drivers and public data
- Census BPS single-family permits for the counties where it builds (Paulding, Bartow GA; Johnston NC; Rutherford TN; Shelby AL; and others).
- County planning-board plat and subdivision approvals naming Smith Douglas communities.
- 10-Q: homes closed, net new orders, average selling price, lots controlled, gross margin.

## Kill criteria
- Single-family permits in its core counties fall more than 20% year over year for two consecutive months while its order growth turns negative.
- Gross margin compression of more than 300 bps year over year from incentives.
- Lots controlled decline two quarters in a row (pipeline shrinking).

## Open questions
- Incentive levels by division; mortgage-rate sensitivity of first-time buyers.
